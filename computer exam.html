Computer

Introduction

A computer is an electronic machine that is used to store, process and display information. It helps us perform many tasks Quickly and accurately. Computers are used in schools, offices, banks, hospitals, railway stations and homes. we can use computers for studying, typing, drawing, playing games and accessing the internet.

Basic Parts of a Computer

Parts Functions
Monitor Displays information
CPU Processes data
Keyboard Used for typing
Mouse Used for pointing and clicking
Printer Prints documents
Speakers Produce sound
